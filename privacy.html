Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Who we are

This website is run by AI Video Strategy (Vasiliki Liggou, Athens, Greece). We are the data controller for the personal data described below. Contact: aivideostrategy@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect and why

  • Free ebook sign-up: your first name and email address. We use them to send you the ebook, tips about AI video, and offers for our products and services. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Contact, email and booking: if you email us or book a call, we receive the information you send (name, email, details about your project). We use it to reply and to provide the service you asked for. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.
  • Purchases: when you buy an ebook or course, the payment provider processes your payment details and gives us your name, email and order details. We use them to deliver the product, keep accounting records and provide support. Legal basis: performance of a contract and legal obligations. We never see your full card number.
  • Basic technical data: like most sites, our hosting provider may log your IP address and browser information for security and operation.

3. Cookies and local storage

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. We store your chosen display currency in your browser’s local storage, only so the page remembers it. Third-party services embedded on the site (see below) may set their own cookies when they load, for example the Calendly booking calendar.

4. Who we share data with

We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf or under their own policies:

  • OGI Marketing System: email list and email delivery.
  • Calendly: booking calls (loads only on the Contact page).
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting.
  • Google Fonts: loads the fonts used on this site, which means your IP address is sent to Google.
  • Frankfurter (frankfurter.dev): public exchange-rate service used to show prices in other currencies. No personal data is sent apart from your IP address.
  • Our payment provider: payments and delivery of purchases. The provider’s own privacy policy applies to your payment data.

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, transfers rely on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your personal data.

5. How long we keep it

Email subscribers: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data. Enquiries: as long as needed to answer and follow up, usually up to 24 months. Order and invoice records: as long as tax law requires.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. You can withdraw consent at any time, and you can unsubscribe using the link in every email. To use these rights, email aivideostrategy@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority; in Greece this is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
