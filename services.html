Questions

How long does a video take?

It depends on the length, the type of video and the level of difficulty. On average, I spend about 5 hours of work per video. Delivery takes 3-4 days for a Single Ad and 7-9 days for a Campaign Pack.

Do I need to send footage?

No. I can create the video from scratch. If you have product photos or a logo, send them and I’ll use them.

What if I want changes?

Each package includes one round of revisions. Extra rounds can be added.

Can I use the videos in paid ads?

Yes, but we agree on it first. Tell me where you want to run the videos and I’ll confirm the usage details with your quote.

How do I pay?

You can pay by bank transfer, PayPal or a card payment link. We agree on the payment details together before we start.
